---
name: ripple-checkup
description: Use when the user says "ripple checkup", "check the live feed", or asks to review, clean up or fix Ripple's live loop. Reviews the free live robot's recent calls, fixes misreads in the rulebook, repairs failed runs, and leaves a checkup note.
---

Follow `docs/checkup.md` in this repo step by step. Work from the repo root with `.venv/bin/python`.

Report back in plain words: whether the loop is healthy, what it got wrong and the rule you added for each,
how many calls you withdrew and why, and the current score. Keep the note you leave on the site to one
sentence a non-expert understands.
